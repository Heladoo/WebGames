Anonymous counts only: visits, estimated unique visitors, and which games were opened from the homepage. Game ideas sent by players wait for you below.

Set up statistics (free, about 5 minutes)

  1. Create a free account at console.upstash.com.
  2. In the Redis tab choose Create Database, pick the Free plan and the region closest to your players.
  3. Open the database and copy UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL and UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_TOKEN from its REST API section.
  4. In Vercel: your project → Settings → Environment Variables. Add those two, plus STATS_KEY with a secret phrase of your choice (all environments).
  5. Redeploy (Deployments → ⋯ → Redeploy). Variables only apply to new deployments.
  6. Come back to this page and enter your STATS_KEY.

No accounts, cookies or personal data are involved in these counts. The same database also holds the game ideas players send; they are shown only here, to you.
